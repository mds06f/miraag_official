The philosophy

An old soul.
A new sound.

MIRAAG is born from the meeting of two timeless ideas: the spirit of Mirabai and the soul of the raag.

MIRA draws inspiration from Mirabai, whose songs carried the language of love, devotion and artistic freedom. Her music was more than performance: it was an expression of an inner world.

RAAG is the living architecture of Indian classical music — a melodic world with its own character, emotion and time.

MIRAAG brings these inspirations into a contemporary musical space. Classical melodies meet electric guitars; the depth of Indian musical heritage encounters the raw energy of rock. The result is not a departure from tradition, but a new way of carrying its spirit forward.

“A meeting of devotion and rebellion, heritage and reinvention.”

We don't just perform music. We reinvent its legacy.
